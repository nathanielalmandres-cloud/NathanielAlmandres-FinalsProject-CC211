DINE WITH YOUR FURRY FRIENDS

Welcome to Ne-kohi, a pet friendly cafe that serves both you and your furry companion. Our cafe offers aesthetic scenery that both you along with peers and pets can relax, have fun and enjoy our finest foods and drinks. Our cafe is unlike any other, not only we can serve you but we also serve your companions giving them treats so both of you can enjoy your stay at our cafe.

OUR STORY

Our business started at a small rented place with fewer customers because its unique architecture made them feel anxious whether to come or not because they thought we sell expensive products.

Not until they found out that we don't care about profit thus we only care about making people smile along with their companion, our business care about the happiness we bring to our customers by serving them efficiently along with respect to them and having a pet friendly environment.

WHAT WE OFFER

Our business offers wide variety of foods such as pastries, pastas and other snacks. We also offer drinks from coffees, milkteas and as well as fruit drinks. Besides foods and drinks for our customers, we also sell treats for their pets following strict dietary composition to ensure their health. Not only that our shop also have board games and books to provide entertainment to our customers and toys for their pets.
